Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The Method Home ("The Method Home," "we," "us," or "our") provides custom closet, pantry, and wardrobe organization design and installation services in New York City and Orlando, FL. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through themethodhome.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, primarily through our quote request form. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • The type of space you'd like organized and any details you share about your project (room size, timeline, pain points, etc.)

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect payment or financial information through the Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your quote request and schedule consultations or appointments
  • Communicate with you about your project
  • Improve our services and how we present them on the Site

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site or process form submissions (for example, email delivery or form-processing tools), solely so we can receive and respond to your inquiry
  • Our co-founders and staff involved in fulfilling your project
  • Authorities, if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others

Cookies and Analytics

At this time, the Site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tracking in the future (for example, to understand site traffic), we will update this policy to describe what is collected and how you can opt out.

Data Retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, complete any project you engage us for, and maintain business records, after which we delete or anonymize it unless we're required to keep it longer by law.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us at the address below. We'll respond as promptly as we can.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at:

  • New York: InfoNY@themethodhome.com
  • Orlando, FL: InfoFL@themethodhome.com
