Questions

NYC questions, answered.

Which parts of NYC do you serve?

Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens and the surrounding metro, including the Upper East Side, Park Slope and Williamsburg. If you're elsewhere, ask.

Do you work in rentals?

Yes. Many of our systems are freestanding or removable. Mention it's a rental when you ask for a quote and we'll plan around it.

Can you work around my building's rules?

Yes. Tell us your building's rules, like elevator reservations or allowed work hours, when you request a quote, and we'll plan install day around them.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the space and the finishes you pick. After a free walkthrough you get one fixed price before any work starts.

How long does an install take?

Most single rooms, like one closet or a pantry, take one day. Whole-home projects can take a few days.

Do I need to be home for the walkthrough?

Yes, so we can see how you use the space. Install day can often happen while you're out.
