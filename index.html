Common Questions

Before you reach out.

How much does a custom system cost?

It depends on the size of the space, materials, and finish you choose. You'll get a fixed, no-surprises quote after your in-home walkthrough, before any work begins.

How long does an install take?

Most single-room projects, like one closet or a pantry, are installed in a single day. Whole-home projects may take a few days depending on scope.

Do you offer help if I'm not ready for a full install?

Yes. If you just want ideas, we offer remote consultations, an hour or two with us to talk through your space and get professional direction on how to make it feel elevated, no onsite visit required.

Do I need to be home for the walkthrough?

Yes. We measure the space and talk through how you actually use it, which is hard to do without you there. Install day can sometimes be scheduled while you're out, depending on access.

Do you work with rentals or apartments?

Yes. Many of our systems can be built freestanding or designed to be removable. Mention it's a rental when you request a quote and we'll plan around it.

How far in advance should I book?

It varies by season, so the sooner you request a quote, the sooner we can get you on the schedule. Most requests get a same-week response.
