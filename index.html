Questions

Before you reach out.

How much does a custom system cost?

It depends on the size of the space and the finishes you pick. After a free walkthrough you get one fixed price before any work starts.

How long does an install take?

Most single rooms, like one closet or a pantry, take one day. Whole-home projects can take a few days.

Can you help if I'm not ready for a full install?

Yes. Book a remote consultation: one or two hours on video where we look at your space and tell you what to change.

Do I need to be home for the walkthrough?

Yes, so we can see how you use the space. Install day can often happen while you're out.

Do you work with rentals or apartments?

Yes. Many of our systems are freestanding or removable. Mention it's a rental and we'll plan around it.

How far ahead should I book?

It varies by season. Most requests get a reply the same week, and we schedule the walkthrough from there.
